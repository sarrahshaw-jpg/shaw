# Setting up Noor — for Ali

Noor is your executive assistant. She lives inside Claude, on your phone and laptop. You talk to her in ordinary words. This takes about 20 minutes, once.

You will need: the Claude app, the 10 files attached to this message, and your logins for Google, your email, Zoho and Zoom.

---

## 1. Give her to your Claude, 5 minutes

On a computer, at claude.ai:

**Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload skill.** Upload all 10 files, one at a time. Start with `executive-assistant`. That is Noor herself. The nine `ea-` files are the things she knows how to do.

That is the whole installation. She is now part of your Claude, in every conversation, on every device you sign into.

## 2. Connect what she works with, 5 minutes

**Settings → Connectors.** Connect these five:

- Google Drive
- Google Calendar, your own
- Hostinger Mail, your `ar@alirao.com` mailbox
- Zoho CRM
- Zoom for Claude

Only you can do this step. Connecting signs in as you, so nobody can do it on your behalf. Without these she has nothing to read.

## 3. Give her a memory, 2 minutes

A Google Drive folder called **`EA — Ali Rao`** has been prepared for you and shared with your Google account. Open it once and confirm you can edit it. That folder is where she keeps your preferences, your important contacts, what you have promised people, the approvals waiting for you, and a record of everything she has done. It is how she is the same assistant tomorrow as she was today.

## 4. Make her work while you are not looking, 5 minutes

This is the part that makes her an assistant rather than a search box. At claude.ai → **Scheduled tasks → New**, three times. The exact text to paste into each is in the three files your admin has.

| | When, Dubai time |
|---|---|
| Morning briefing | 10:00 |
| Check through the day | every hour, 11:00 to 21:00 |
| Evening wrap | 21:30 |

Turn on **notifications on completion** for each one. These must be created while signed in as you, because a scheduled task uses the connectors of whoever made it.

Nothing runs outside those hours. She will not wake you at midnight.

## 5. Try her

Type or say: **"Noor, what do I have today?"**

If your schedule comes back, everything works.

---

## How to use her

Just talk. No commands, no menus.

- "What's important in my inbox?"
- "Prepare me for my next meeting"
- "Find time with Ahmed next week"
- "Draft a reply to Khalid saying Thursday works" then "Read it to me" then "Send it"
- "What did we agree with ABC last time?"
- "Which clients need me?"
- "What did you do today?"

Say "Noor" on its own and she answers and waits. Once you are talking to her you do not need the name again.

**She never sends anything without you.** No email goes out, no meeting with an outsider is moved, nothing is written into Zoho, until she shows you exactly what she proposes and you say yes. If you do not say send, a draft just waits for you. The overnight and hourly runs cannot send anything at all, by design.

**By voice**, tap the voice button in the app and speak. She answers in short spoken sentences rather than lists. Before sending anything you approved by ear but never saw on screen, she reads it back to you word for word and asks once more. That is deliberate.

## Two things she cannot do, so you are not surprised

She reacts hourly, not instantly. A meeting that ends at 14:05 is followed up by 15:00, not at 14:06.

There is no "Hey Noor" from a locked phone. That is an Apple and Google feature, not something Claude offers. You open the app, then speak.
