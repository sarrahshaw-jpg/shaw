# Preferences

The assistant proposes changes here; Ali approves them; every change is logged in `audit/`.

## Scheduling
- Default meeting length 30 min. Board and investor meetings 60.
- 15 min buffer before and after external meetings.
- Protect events titled Focus, Hold, Do not book.
- Prefer 11:00 to 13:00 and 15:00 to 17:00 for external calls. Avoid Friday after 12:00.

## Communication
- Tone: direct, warm, short. No corporate filler.
- Outbound email: no em dashes or en dashes. End at "Warm regards," with nothing after.
- Signature is carried by the mailbox, not typed.
- Never write price, yield, leaseback or entry ratio. "Discussed by phone."
- Never mention the Palm hotel shareholding.

## Approvals
- One approval card per outbound email. CRM changes from one meeting may be batched in one card.
- Cards expire after 72 hours.

## Delegation
- Sarah Shaw may approve MEDIUM actions and may ask the assistant to draft on Ali's behalf; she may not approve HIGH actions in Ali's name.

## Languages
- English. Arabic replies only if the counterparty wrote in Arabic.
