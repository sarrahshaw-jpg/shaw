# Editing Style Guidelines (Sarah Signature)

## Core Philosophy
The editing must feel **expensive, psychological, intelligent, elegant, and modern.** 
It should *never* look like an over-edited gaming video.

## Specifications

### 1. Visual Quality
- **Framing:** Clean headroom, natural eye-line. Vertical 9:16 priority.
- **Lighting:** Simulate studio illumination. Fix underexposure.
- **Color:** Cinematic, true-to-life skin tones. No plastic smoothing.

### 2. Pacing & Cuts
- **Cadence:** Maintain human breathing and natural pauses. 
- **Fillers:** Remove "ums", "uhs", and false starts seamlessly.
- **Silence:** Cut long awkward dead air, but leave dramatic pauses before key statements.

### 3. Dynamic Zoom (Multi-Cam Illusion)
- Only zoom on semantic shifts (a new point, a strong conclusion).
- Scales: `100% -> 110% -> 118%`.
- Cuts between zoom levels should be clean or have extremely subtle, slow push-ins.

### 4. Captions & Text
- **Style:** Minimal, premium, sans-serif fonts.
- **Grouping:** Group by natural phrasing, not raw word-by-word vomit.
- **Emphasis:** Highlight the most important keyword in a sentence (e.g., color shift or weight change).

### 5. Sound Design
- **Music:** Subtle, modern, atmospheric. Never overpowers the voice.
- **SFX:** Restrained. Soft whooshes or minimal clicks for text appearance.

## Identity Protection
- **NEVER** alter Sarah's face, features, or voice. Enhancement is permitted; synthesis/alteration is strictly forbidden.
